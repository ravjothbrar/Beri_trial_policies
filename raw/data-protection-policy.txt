DATA PROTECTION POLICY
Haberdashers' Elstree Schools

Introduction

Haberdashers' Elstree Schools is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal information. This policy sets out how the school handles personal data in compliance with the Data Protection Act 2018 and the UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy applies to all staff, governors, students, parents, and any other individuals whose personal data is processed by the school.

What is Personal Data?

Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual. This includes:

• Names and contact details
• Date of birth
• Photographs and video recordings
• Academic records and assessment data
• Attendance records
• Medical information
• Behaviour and exclusion records
• Special educational needs information
• Free school meal eligibility
• Biometric data (where used)

The GDPR Principles

The school will process personal data in accordance with the six data protection principles:

1. Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency - Personal data shall be processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner.

2. Purpose Limitation - Personal data shall be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and not further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.

3. Data Minimisation - Personal data shall be adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes for which it is processed.

4. Accuracy - Personal data shall be accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date.

5. Storage Limitation - Personal data shall be kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects for no longer than is necessary.

6. Integrity and Confidentiality - Personal data shall be processed in a manner that ensures appropriate security, including protection against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

Data Subject Rights

Under the GDPR, individuals have the following rights regarding their personal data:

• The right to be informed about how their data is being used
• The right of access to their personal data
• The right to have their data corrected if it is inaccurate
• The right to have their data erased in certain circumstances
• The right to restrict processing of their data
• The right to data portability
• The right to object to processing
• Rights related to automated decision-making and profiling

To exercise any of these rights, individuals should contact the school's Data Protection Officer.

Lawful Basis for Processing

The school processes personal data under the following lawful bases:

• Consent - where the individual has given clear consent for the school to process their personal data for a specific purpose
• Contract - where processing is necessary for a contract the school has with the individual
• Legal obligation - where processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation
• Vital interests - where processing is necessary to protect someone's life
• Public task - where processing is necessary to perform a task in the public interest or for official functions
• Legitimate interests - where processing is necessary for the legitimate interests of the school

For special category data (such as health information, ethnicity, or biometric data), the school will also identify an additional lawful basis from those set out in GDPR Article 9.

Data Security Requirements

The school takes the security of personal data seriously. The following measures are in place:

Physical Security:
• All paper records containing personal data are kept in locked filing cabinets
• Access to school buildings is restricted and monitored
• Visitors must sign in and wear identification badges
• Clear desk policy for areas where personal data is processed

IT Security:
• All computers are password protected
• Passwords must be strong and changed regularly
• Data is encrypted where appropriate
• Regular backups are performed
• Anti-virus software is kept up to date
• Firewalls protect the school network
• Staff are trained in data security practices

Data Sharing

The school may share personal data with:

• The Department for Education (DfE)
• Ofsted
• Local authorities
• NHS health professionals
• Educational psychologists
• Police and law enforcement agencies
• School trip organisers and venues
• Examination boards
• Universities and employers (with student consent)

Personal data will only be shared when there is a lawful basis to do so, and appropriate safeguards are in place.

Data Breaches

A data breach is any incident that results in unauthorised access to, or loss, alteration, or disclosure of personal data.

If a data breach occurs, staff must immediately report it to the Data Protection Officer. The school will investigate the breach and, where necessary, report it to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) within 72 hours.

If the breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, the school will also inform those affected.

Staff Responsibilities

All staff are responsible for:

• Only accessing personal data that they need to perform their role
• Not sharing personal data inappropriately
• Keeping personal data secure
• Reporting any suspected data breaches immediately
• Completing data protection training
• Following the school's data protection procedures

Data Protection Officer

The school's Data Protection Officer is Mrs Emma Thompson. She can be contacted at dpo@habselstree.org.uk or 020 8123 4567.

The Data Protection Officer is responsible for:

• Monitoring compliance with data protection legislation
• Advising on data protection impact assessments
• Cooperating with the ICO
• Acting as the point of contact for data subjects and the ICO

Retention of Data

The school will only retain personal data for as long as necessary. Retention periods vary depending on the type of data:

• Student academic records - retained until the student reaches age 25
• Safeguarding records - retained until the student reaches age 25, or longer if concerns are serious
• Attendance registers - retained for 3 years
• Staff employment records - retained for 6 years after employment ends
• Accident reports - retained for 21 years

Full details are available in the school's Data Retention Schedule.

Privacy Notices

The school provides privacy notices to students, parents, and staff explaining:

• What personal data is collected
• Why it is collected
• How it will be used
• Who it will be shared with
• How long it will be kept
• Individual rights

Privacy notices are available on the school website and are provided when individuals first join the school.

Monitoring and Review

This policy will be reviewed annually by the Senior Leadership Team and the Board of Governors.
