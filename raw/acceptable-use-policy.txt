ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY - STUDENTS
Haberdashers' Elstree Schools

Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is designed to ensure that all students use the school's ICT facilities and internet access in a safe, responsible, and appropriate manner. All students and their parents/carers are required to sign the Student ICT Agreement before being granted access to school ICT resources.

This policy applies to all use of ICT facilities provided by the school, including:

• Desktop computers and laptops
• Tablets and other mobile devices
• School network and internet access
• School email accounts
• Virtual Learning Environment (VLE)
• Any other school-provided ICT equipment or services

General Use

Students are expected to:

• Use school ICT resources for educational purposes only
• Respect and take care of all ICT equipment
• Report any damage or faults to a member of staff immediately
• Keep their login details secure and not share passwords with others
• Log off or lock computers when leaving them unattended
• Be polite and respectful in all online communications
• Follow copyright laws and acknowledge sources of information
• Only use software and apps approved by the school

Access and Security

Students must:

• Only log in using their own username and password
• Never attempt to access another person's account or files
• Never attempt to bypass school security settings or filters
• Not attempt to access or modify system settings
• Report any security concerns or breaches to a member of staff immediately
• Use only their school email account for school-related communications

Students must not:

• Share their passwords with anyone, including friends
• Use another student's account
• Attempt to access staff accounts or systems
• Try to access restricted areas of the school network
• Install or download unauthorised software

Internet Use

Students are expected to use the internet responsibly and appropriately. The school provides filtered internet access to help protect students, but no filter is 100% effective.

Acceptable internet use includes:

• Researching information for schoolwork
• Accessing educational websites and resources
• Using the school VLE and approved learning platforms
• Communicating with teachers via school email for educational purposes

Unacceptable internet use includes:

• Accessing or attempting to access inappropriate websites
• Accessing social media sites unless for a specific educational task supervised by a teacher
• Online shopping or gambling
• Accessing or distributing material that is offensive, obscene, or illegal
• Downloading or streaming music, videos, or games for personal entertainment
• Using the internet to bully, harass, or intimidate others

Email and Communication

Students should:

• Use school email accounts for school-related communications only
• Be polite and respectful in all emails and online communications
• Use appropriate language and tone
• Think carefully before sending messages or posting content online
• Remember that emails and online communications can be monitored by the school

Students should not:

• Send or forward chain emails, spam, or junk mail
• Open emails or attachments from unknown senders
• Share personal information online (such as home address, phone number, or photos)
• Use email to send inappropriate, offensive, or hurtful messages
• Impersonate others in email or online communications

Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited and may result in serious sanctions:

• Cyberbullying - sending threatening, abusive, or hurtful messages online
• Accessing, creating, storing, or distributing offensive, obscene, or illegal material
• Hacking or attempting to access unauthorised systems or accounts
• Deliberately introducing viruses or other malware
• Attempting to bypass security measures or internet filters
• Plagiarism or academic dishonesty
• Using ICT to engage in illegal activities
• Downloading or distributing copyrighted material without permission

Use of Personal Devices

Students may only use personal devices (such as mobile phones, tablets, or laptops) in school in accordance with the school's Mobile Phone Policy and E-Safety Policy. Personal devices must not be used to:

• Record (photo, video, or audio) other students or staff without explicit permission
• Access inappropriate content
• Disrupt lessons or school activities
• Bully, harass, or intimidate others

Sanctions

Breaches of this Acceptable Use Policy will be taken seriously and may result in:

• Loss of internet or computer access privileges
• Confiscation of devices
• Detention
• Contact with parents/carers
• In serious cases, suspension or permanent exclusion
• In cases of illegal activity, referral to the police

The school reserves the right to monitor internet and email usage to ensure compliance with this policy and to protect the safety of students and staff.

Data Protection and Privacy

Students should be aware that:

• The school may monitor internet usage and email communications
• Inappropriate use may be investigated and evidence may be used in disciplinary proceedings
• The school will respect student privacy but reserves the right to access accounts if there are safeguarding or security concerns

Responsibilities

Students are responsible for:

• Their own behaviour online, both in and out of school
• Reporting any concerns about online safety to a trusted adult
• Following the guidance in this policy at all times

Parents/carers are responsible for:

• Discussing online safety with their children
• Monitoring their child's internet use at home
• Supporting the school in promoting responsible ICT use

Staff are responsible for:

• Modelling good practice in ICT use
• Teaching students about online safety and responsible ICT use
• Monitoring student use of ICT and reporting concerns
• Enforcing the Acceptable Use Policy
